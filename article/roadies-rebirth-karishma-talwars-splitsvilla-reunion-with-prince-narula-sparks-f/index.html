Roadies Rebirth: Karishma Talwar's Splitsvilla Reunion With Prince Narula Sparks Fan Edits

A viral Roadies Rebirth promo reunited former Splitsvilla 8 contestants Karishma Talwar and Prince Narula, prompting him to publicly clarify she was never his ex and ask fans to stop pairing them in edits.

AITeaPost AI• just now • 2 min read

A promo clip from MTV's new season Roadies Rebirth, which premiered on October 16, 2026, has gone viral for reuniting two former Splitsvilla 8 contestants on screen — Karishma Talwar and gang leader Prince Narula — more than a decade after their on-screen connection on the dating reality show, according to multiple entertainment outlets.

What happened

In the clip, shared widely on Instagram and picked up by NewsX and other outlets, Karishma Talwar is seen confronting Prince Narula during an audition round, telling him "You ditched me." Host Rannvijay Singha and fellow gang leader Neha Dhupia join in on the banter. According to a recap by The Daily Jagran, Karishma jokingly claimed Prince left her because he believed she had "sold him for Rs 599," and at one point called him "sexy" — prompting Neha Dhupia to tease him about rekindling the old connection. Karishma said the remark was only a compliment before announcing she would join the show's "OG" team.

Her presence on the show itself is not fully confirmed. Business Upturn reported on October 13 that Karishma was "reportedly joining Roadies Rebirth as a contestant," citing a social media update from the account Reality Scoop, but noted the show's makers had not independently confirmed her participation. Other outlets, including Tellychakkar, described her as having auditioned for the season.

Prince Narula's response

The clip quickly spawned fan edits pairing the two as a couple, which led Prince Narula to issue a public clarification. "Guys it's a humble request," he wrote, according to the Free Press Journal, asking fans to stop making such edits and to "remember that I have a wife and daughter." He added: "I'm a happily married man and I don't want my daughter to grow up and watch these meaningless edits."

Narula was explicit that the characterisation of Karishma as his former partner was inaccurate: "she was simply a 'connection' in the villa, not an ex, as is being shown," he said, while calling his years on reality television "a beautiful journey." India Forums carried a similar version of his statement, confirming he was pushing back specifically on the "ex" framing rather than denying the on-screen moment itself.

Background

Splitsvilla 8 aired in 2015, hosted by Sunny Leone and Rannvijay Singha. Prince Narula won that season alongside partner Anuki Tchokhonelidze; Karishma Talwar, a reality TV personality and events anchor, finished as second runner-up alongside contestant Zaan Khan. The two were never a winning pair, though outlets covering the Roadies Rebirth reunion note their arcs intersected during the season. Narula went on to win MTV Roadies X2 and later Bigg Boss 9, before returning to Roadies this year as one of three "OG" gang leaders — alongside Nikhil Chinapa and Neha Dhupia — facing a "NewG" team of Abhishek Malhan, Bani J and Baseer Ali in the rebooted season's format.

Reports on Karishma Talwar's personal details, including her marital status, have been inconsistent across outlets, so TeaPost has not included them here.
