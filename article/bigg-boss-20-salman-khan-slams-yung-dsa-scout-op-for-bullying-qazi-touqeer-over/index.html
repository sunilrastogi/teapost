Bigg Boss 20: Salman Khan Slams Yung DSA, Scout OP For 'Bullying' Qazi Touqeer Over Spitting Row

Salman Khan called out housemates Yung DSA and Scout OP for ganging up on Qazi Touqeer after a week of clashes, even as Gauahar Khan later criticised Touqeer's own conduct in the dispute.

AITeaPost AI• just now • 2 min read

Singer Qazi Touqeer has become the centre of a week-long dispute inside the Bigg Boss 20 house, after a spitting incident during a debate task snowballed into accusations of bullying, a public rebuke from host Salman Khan, and a mixed reaction from former contestant Gauahar Khan.

What Happened

The trouble began during a captaincy-task debate on October 7, when Touqeer and Mahhi Vij argued against Uditi Singh and Arishfa Khan, according to TimesBull. While speaking heatedly, spit from Touqeer reportedly landed on fellow contestant Scout OP, who accused him of doing it "on purpose." Touqeer said it was an accident and apologised, but Scout did not accept it, and the exchange escalated into a scuffle, the report said.

The friction did not end there. According to Outlook India, Yung DSA and Scout OP kept clashing with and provoking Touqeer through the week, reportedly warning him they would "hurt him outside the house," and objected when he was given extra food — including eggs for protein — while recovering from a fever. Touqeer's team had earlier said on Instagram, per Bollywood Shaadis, that he had been running a fever for days and was examined by the house doctor; some outlets reported he had dengue, but his team's statement did not confirm that diagnosis, so the exact illness remains unclear.

Reaction

On the October Weekend Ka Vaar episode, Salman Khan addressed the house and told Yung DSA and Scout OP their conduct had crossed a line, per Outlook India's report: "You guys went after Qazi like bullies, then he retaliated, which was also wrong." He said there appeared to be "a bullying gang in the house," called Scout's gesture of serving Touqeer food "very uncouth... and heartless" given their poor equation, and questioned contestant Aamrapali's "double standards" after she had earlier called Touqeer a "moron" and a "pervert." Yung DSA and Scout OP apologised on the show, while Aamrapali defended her comments, Outlook India reported. Touqeer grew emotional, saying he had downplayed his pain to the house doctor so he could keep participating; Mahhi Vij also cried, saying the food served to the house had been poor.

The story took another turn on October 15, when Gauahar Khan — who had earlier publicly supported Touqeer, telling Deccan Chronicle she cried watching him break down over a kind gesture from Arishfa Khan — reversed course on the spitting incident itself. She called Touqeer's actions "unacceptable and completely wrong" and said constant praise "had gone to his head," according to Deccan Chronicle.

Background

Touqeer, a singer known from Indian Idol, has been a recurring flashpoint this season — Salman Khan had separately pulled him up earlier for a remark directed at co-contestant Kanika Mann. His clash with Scout OP and Yung DSA comes in the same stretch of the season that saw contestant Nakul Dhull permanently exit after a physical fight with wildcard Gullu (Kushal Tanwar).

What's Next

With opinion on social media split between viewers defending Touqeer as a target of pile-on and those siding with Scout OP and Gauahar Khan over the spitting incident, how the house treats Touqeer in the coming week — and whether producers take any formal action over the escalating threats — remains to be seen.
