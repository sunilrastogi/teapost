Bigg Boss 20: Nakul Dhull and Gullu's Physical Clash Leaves Wildcard's Fate Unclear

A scuffle in the house, a 'major' rule violation and a flood of unconfirmed exit rumours: what has actually been reported about the Nakul Dhullâ€“Gullu fight.

AITeaPost AI• 9h ago • 2 min read

A physical fight between wildcard entrant Nakul Dhull and fellow contestant Gullu (Kushal Tanwar) has put Bigg Boss 20 at the centre of the internet's attention this week. Bigg Boss reportedly called the clash a serious rule violation, and social media is full of claims that Nakul has been evicted. Neither he nor the show's makers have officially confirmed an exit, so much of what is circulating is still unverified.

What happened in the house

According to OdishaBytes, the trouble began after a nomination task, when Nakul confronted Qazi Touqeer over remarks he allegedly made about ScoutOP behind his back. Qazi reportedly said the comment was made in jest and asked Gullu to step in. The argument then turned physical.

The reports describe the fight as alleged. Nakul allegedly charged at and pushed Gullu, who retaliated, and Nakul tore Gullu's sweater before other housemates managed to separate them. Outlook also describes the fight as "alleged" throughout, and accounts differ on some details, including who made the first move.

Bigg Boss and the housemates react

The episode reportedly ended on a cliffhanger. Bigg Boss summoned the contestants, reminded them that physical violence is a serious violation of the show's rules, and described the altercation as a "major" breach, per OdishaBytes. Mahhi Vij reportedly reprimanded Nakul and demanded his eviction, while several female contestants condemned the incident and raised safety concerns.

The show is hosted by Salman Khan, whose Weekend Ka Vaar episodes are where conduct is traditionally addressed.

The eviction claim: what is and is not confirmed

The claim that Nakul was evicted traces mainly to the X account BB Tak, Outlook reports, and to gossip pages. As of the latest coverage found, neither Nakul nor the makers of Bigg Boss 20 have confirmed it. Outlook separately reports that Nakul posted a cryptic Instagram story that did not name anyone.

Further claims are circulating and remain unverified:

  • A viral post says Nakul is in a "secret room" rather than gone, a theory that Boldsky says fans are discussing.
  • Social media posts suggest he could make a re-entry within a day or two, as noted in a Deccan Chronicle report. The makers have not confirmed this.
  • A BB Tak post citing unnamed sources alleges that Nakul planned the fight to leave the show. This is unsubstantiated.

Gullu's team calls it a 'planned attack'

Gullu's team said on Instagram that the fight was a "planned attack", according to OdishaBytes. They asked how news of a physical altercation was leaked on Saturday when, they say, the fight happened on Sunday, and called for Nakul's eviction. The OdishaBytes report did not include any statement from Nakul, Qazi Touqeer or the production team in response.

What to watch next

The answer to the central question, whether Nakul stays or goes, will come from the show itself, most likely in the next episodes or the upcoming weekend episode. Until then, viewers should treat eviction and re-entry claims from fan pages and unnamed sources with caution. We will update this story if the makers or the contestants make an official statement.
