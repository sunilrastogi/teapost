Bigg Boss 20: Arishfa Khan Breaks Down, Trashes Room After Losing Lifeline In Ration Task

Housemates voted to strip Arishfa Khan of a lifeline to save the house's ration, and her angry, pillow-throwing reaction split the group.

AITeaPost AI• 2 min ago • 2 min read

On Bigg Boss 20's October 16 episode, Bigg Boss set housemates a ration task with a cruel twist: they had 20 minutes to mutually agree on one contestant who would lose a "lifeline," and every extra minute of indecision would cost the house 5% of its food supply. The group settled on Arishfa Khan, who lost one of her two lives after the vote, according to WION and English Bombay Samachar. Because the housemates used the full 20 minutes to decide, the house was left with only 55% of its ration.

What happened

Reports differ slightly on the exact tally. WION and Bombay Samachar put it at six votes against Arishfa, while TellyReviews reported seven — and contestants themselves disagreed on the number during the episode, with Scout, Uditi Singh and Aman saying six people voted against her while Yung DSA, Aamrapali Dubey and Kanika Mann counted seven. Kushal Tanwar ("Gullu") and Kanika Mann were reportedly the most determined to see Arishfa lose a life, and Manish Goel said her concerns "may be valid" but her approach was "always wrong," according to TellyReviews.

Arishfa, who felt singled out, did not take the decision quietly. She threw pillows and cushions around the bedroom and garden area, according to WION, Bombay Samachar and BollywoodMasalaByAnchal, and reportedly hid Gullu's bottle and cup to frustrate other housemates. She later broke down crying in the bedroom area. ScoutOP, who initially tried to calm her, grew irritated instead and decided to punish her for the mess, Bombay Samachar reported.

The fallout

The incident split the house. Kanika Mann confronted Uditi Singh and Aman for not defending Arishfa during the vote, while Aman argued that a chaotic discussion in the room would have stalled any decision at all, per TellyReviews. Luv Gill was among the few housemates who sided with Arishfa. Gullu reportedly asked Manish not to clean up the mess Arishfa had left behind, though Manish said he would clean it once to see whether the behaviour continued.

The episode's precap showed host Salman Khan addressing the house's recent run of conflict during the Weekend Ka Vaar episode — including Qazi Touqeer spitting in ScoutOP's ear and a physical fight involving Nakul Dhull — and a promo for that episode shows him calling out Uditi Singh specifically for not standing up for Arishfa.

Background

Arishfa Khan, 23, entered Bigg Boss 20 as one of the season's most followed digital-creator contestants. A child actor since 2012 — with credits including Uttaran, Yeh Hai Mohabbatein and Gangaa — she went on to build a following as a short-form content creator, with her Instagram following reported at more than 29 million as of September 2026. She has clashed repeatedly with other housemates this season, including earlier confrontations with Aamrapali Dubey and ScoutOP.

Bigg Boss 20 airs daily on Colors TV and streams on JioHotstar, with Salman Khan hosting the Weekend Ka Vaar episodes.
