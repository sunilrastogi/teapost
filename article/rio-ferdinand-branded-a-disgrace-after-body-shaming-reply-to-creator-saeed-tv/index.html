Rio Ferdinand Branded A 'Disgrace' After Body-Shaming Reply To Creator Saeed TV

The former Manchester United defender faced online backlash after mocking content creator Saeed TV's weight when challenged to criticise the club's owners.

AITeaPost AI• 8h ago • 2 min read

Former Manchester United defender and football pundit Rio Ferdinand is facing backlash after appearing to mock the physique of Manchester United content creator Saeed, known online as Saeed TV, in an Instagram comments exchange on October 16, 2026, according to Manchester United fan outlet centredevils.co.uk and the sports aggregator OneFootball, which cited Goal.com.

What happened

The exchange began under an Instagram post in which Ferdinand praised current United manager Michael Carrick, centredevils.co.uk reported. Saeed TV, who has been critical of United's ownership amid the club's poor season, commented underneath it.

"Stop yapping and call out the owners!"

— Saeed TV, on Instagram, via centredevils.co.uk

Ferdinand replied through his verified account with a remark mocking Saeed's weight, telling him to "pick up ya ts" and suggesting he was "out of breath" while typing, centredevils.co.uk reported. OneFootball, citing Goal.com, reported the same exchange, quoting Ferdinand as writing: "Stop yapping and pick up ya ts, ya out a breath typing!" Both outlets, along with a wider aggregation tracked by Ground News that pulled in reporting from Daily Mail and Daily Express, described the reply as a fat-shaming remark that quickly drew criticism online.

Reaction

According to OneFootball's reporting, Saeed TV initially called Ferdinand a "weirdo" and said the former defender had resorted to personal insults rather than engage with the criticism, adding that it reflected poorly on him. He later posted a longer video response arguing that, as a public figure watched by children, Ferdinand's comment was embarrassing, and said he had developed thick skin after enduring racial abuse in the past, the outlet reported.

Reaction in Ferdinand's own comments section was split: some users branded him a "disgrace," per the Ground News aggregation of Daily Mail and Daily Express coverage, while OneFootball reported that other users defended him. Multiple outlets have since picked up the story as a flashpoint in wider supporter frustration with Manchester United's ownership and results this season.

Background

The clash comes amid mounting fan unrest over Manchester United's form under Carrick, whose side had won only once in their opening nine Premier League matches at the time of writing, according to centredevils.co.uk. Days earlier, defender Lisandro Martinez had publicly criticised the club's "hierarchy" after a defeat to Atletico Madrid before apologising, the outlet reported — part of a tense backdrop in which some supporters have questioned why United-aligned pundits, Ferdinand included, have been reluctant to criticise the club's ownership directly.

What's next

As of this report, neither Ferdinand nor Saeed TV had posted a further public statement beyond the initial exchange and Saeed's video response. Attention is expected to remain on whether Ferdinand addresses or apologises for the remark in the coming days, particularly given his public profile as a pundit.
