Rise and Fall 2: Shehzad Poonawalla's Ultimate Ruler Reign Ends as Sara Gurpal Clashes With Gautam Gulati

Shehzad Poonawalla's run as Ultimate Ruler on Rise and Fall 2 came to an end this week, and the same stretch of episodes saw Sara Gurpal and Gautam Gulati clash over a dining-table outburst that Gurpal said stirred memories of an abusive relationship.

AITeaPost AI• 18 min ago • 2 min read

The power equation on Amazon Prime Video and MX Player's Rise and Fall 2 has shifted again, with political commentator Shehzad Poonawalla's stint as the house's "Ultimate Ruler" coming to a close in the latest run of episodes. The same stretch also produced one of the season's sharper personal confrontations, between Punjabi singer-actor Sara Gurpal and former Bigg Boss winner Gautam Gulati.

What happened

Rise and Fall 2, hosted by Virender Sehwag, runs on a hierarchy in which contestants are split between a privileged "Penthouse" and a harsher "Basement," with weekly power contests deciding who rules over whom. According to NewsBytes, Poonawalla's run as Ultimate Ruler ended during this phase of the season, a shift the outlet reported as reshuffling alliances and the house's overall power balance.

The end of his reign followed a run in which Poonawalla had used the Ultimate Ruler's authority aggressively. Navbharat Live reported that, while still holding the title, he sent Gulati and fellow contestant Raj Grover down to the Basement while protecting Swara Bhasker and Irina Rudakova in the Penthouse — a decision Gulati reportedly responded to by calling Poonawalla a "traitor." Free Press Journal separately reported that the race for the Ultimate Ruler title had narrowed to a final two, Poonawalla and Gulati, after Irina Rudakova was voted out of contention in an earlier round.

The confrontation

In a related episode, Navbharat Live reported that Gulati warned Gurpal and Grover against attempting what he called a "takhta palat," or overthrow, inside the house. The exchange escalated when Gulati, reacting angrily, stomped his foot on the dining table; Gurpal responded by climbing onto the table herself and knocking items off it, according to the outlet.

NewsBytes reported that Gurpal went further in calling out Gulati, telling him she used to respect him but that his conduct in the moment reminded her of an abusive relationship from her past. The outlet described real emotion surfacing in the house as a result, with the confrontation unfolding against an already tense backdrop from a separate rivalry between contestants Kaira Anu and Rishabh Jaiswal. Gulati has not issued any public response to Gurpal's remarks.

Background

The season, which premiered on September 26, has repeatedly turned the Ultimate Ruler contest into the show's flashpoint. In an early episode, LeisureByte reported that evicted contestant Siwet Tomar physically grabbed Poonawalla by the collar after Poonawalla named him the "weakest decision-maker" in that week's Ultimate Ruler race — a title fight that also saw Karan Patel and Swara Bhasker briefly walk out in protest before Tomar was voted off the show. The makers subsequently reminded contestants on-air that physical violence would not be tolerated.

What's next

With Poonawalla no longer Ultimate Ruler, the show has yet to confirm who holds the title now or whether Gulati's position in the house will change as a result. Neither Poonawalla nor Gulati has commented publicly outside the show on either incident.
