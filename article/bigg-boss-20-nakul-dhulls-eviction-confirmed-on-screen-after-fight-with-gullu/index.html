Bigg Boss 20: Nakul Dhull's Eviction Confirmed On Screen After Fight With Gullu

Bigg Boss has shown on air that wildcard contestant Nakul Dhull was evicted for his physical fight with Gullu, resolving days of rumour, though whether he gets a re-entry remains unconfirmed.

AITeaPost AI• 6 min ago • 2 min read

Bigg Boss 20 has resolved days of speculation around wildcard contestant Nakul Dhull's fate: multiple outlets that have now covered the broadcast describe an on-screen confrontation in which Bigg Boss reprimanded Dhull and ordered him out of the house over his physical altercation with fellow contestant Gullu (Kushal Tanwar). The incident had initially circulated only through unconfirmed social media claims; the newer reports say the eviction itself played out on camera.

What happened on screen

According to Leisurebyte's episode recap, the trouble began during a nomination task when Qazi Touqeer told housemates he had bitten and spat in ScoutOP's ear as a prank. Nakul, who overheard the exchange, confronted Qazi on ScoutOP's behalf; when Qazi asked Gullu to explain the joke instead, Nakul objected, and the argument between the two men turned physical. Housemates stepped in to separate them, and Mahhi Vij hit Nakul on the back during the scuffle, the report says.

NewsBytes reported on October 15 that Bigg Boss called the house together, reminded contestants that physical violence is a serious rule violation, and asked Dhull to leave as punishment. A separate Outlook India report on his reaction adds that several housemates, including Mahhi, Kanika Mann, Gullu and Aasif Khan, applauded as he was asked to go, and that Nakul raised his middle finger at them before leaving. Daily Jagran similarly reports that Bigg Boss scolded Nakul in the living room before announcing his elimination, though it still describes the altercation itself as alleged.

Nakul's reaction, and what's still unclear

Outlook reports that shortly after leaving, Dhull posted an Instagram Story reading "apne 2 din >>>> unke 100 din" ("my 2 days are worth more than their 100 days"), without naming anyone directly. He has not given a longer, on-record statement about the incident since.

What remains unresolved is whether Dhull will get a second chance. Social media posts have speculated about a possible re-entry within days, a theory Deccan Chronicle reported but said the makers have not confirmed. Gullu's side had earlier called the fight a "planned attack" on Instagram and pushed for Dhull's removal; there has been no on-record response to that claim from Dhull.

Background

Dhull entered Bigg Boss 20 as a wildcard contestant during a Weekend Ka Vaar episode hosted by Salman Khan, according to Navbharat Live, which reported that he was removed the same day the fight aired — one of the shortest stints of the season. Colors TV and JioHotstar, which air and stream the show respectively, had not issued a separate statement on the eviction as of the latest reports.

TeaPost had earlier reported that the fight's fallout, including the eviction and re-entry claims, was unconfirmed; this update reflects the broadcast outcome as now described by outlets that covered the episode. We will update this story again if Colors, JioHotstar or Dhull issue a direct, on-record statement.
